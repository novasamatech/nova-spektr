Black Spinel, often in varied hues, symbolizes life’s multifaceted journey into mastery, marking transition to protector and leader. Harder than prior materials, it signals the end of student phase and onset of guardianship. This rank refocuses from technical to philosophic, strategic, and socio-political qualities for leadership. Its gem-like resilience reflects inner strength forged through experience, illuminating paths for others. The Free Master embodies wisdom’s spectrum, guiding with insight beyond mere skill.

Masters demonstrate leadership through broader discourse on industry, social, political, and technological contexts. They engage in education and advocacy via articles, media, and events, balancing efforts across current operations, near-term evolution, and long-term direction. Gaining external reputation, they exemplify Polkadot’s values with edge and humility. Promotion via Root call requires majority approval from rank nine, revocable by referendum. Attainment demands six total years of Polkadot as primary focus, plus exhibited qualities in administration, intellect, and social acumen.
